WORKING PRINCIPLES

Useful claims should be inspectable.

01

Separate evidence from inference

State what was observed, what is reported by others, and what remains an interpretation.

02

Calibrate trust

A fluent interface is not proof of accuracy, intent, expertise, or accountability.

03

Keep a verifiable path

For high-impact decisions, preserve logs, sources, review points, and an accountable human owner.

ABOUT KHZ

A small site for clear technical thinking.

This publication distinguishes product language from technical evidence. It does not treat models as persons, and it avoids presenting unverified allegations as fact. Where a claim needs independent corroboration, it is labeled accordingly.
