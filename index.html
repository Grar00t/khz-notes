CURRENT NOTES

Read the record, not the persona.

The publication uses editorial writing for the argument and explicit evidence states for the claims. The two are related, but they are not interchangeable.

WHAT THE SYSTEM IS

AI is a system boundary, not a personality claim.

01

Input

Prompts, documents, retrieved context, events, images, audio, or other data enter the system.

02

Transformation

A model and surrounding software transform those inputs through learned statistical structure, retrieval, rules, tools, filters, or routing.

03

Output

The system emits text, code, classification, recommendation, or an action request. Fluency does not certify truth.

04

Verification

Tests, primary sources, logs, review, provenance, and reproducibility determine whether an output is supportable.

SUBSTITUTION TEST
Replace the persona sentence with the mechanism.“The assistant understands” → “The system generated this output from its inputs and learned statistical structure.” Then ask what was measured, recorded, reviewed, and reproducible.

EVIDENCE LEDGER

A receipt is an evidence object, not a rhetorical flourish.

WORKING PRINCIPLES

Useful claims should be inspectable.

01

Separate evidence from inference

State what was observed, what is reported by others, and what remains an interpretation.

02

Calibrate trust

A fluent interface is not proof of accuracy, intent, expertise, or accountability.

03

Keep a verifiable path

For high-impact decisions, preserve logs, sources, review points, and an accountable human owner.

ABOUT KHZ

A publication for clear technical thinking.

KHZ distinguishes product language from technical evidence. It does not require the reader to believe a persona, a slogan, or an authority claim. Where a statement needs independent corroboration, the uncertainty stays visible.
